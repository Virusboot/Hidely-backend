Privacy Policy for Hidely

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use the Hidely mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide to us directly when creating an account, such as your full name, email address, username, and profile details. Additionally, since Hidely is a location-contextual platform, we collect precise location data with your permission when the app is active to provide nearby place discovery, location-based feeds, and navigation services.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and personalize the features of Hidely (including nearby stays, location discovery, and contextual feeds).
  • Enable voice and video calling features powered by secure real-time communication protocols.
  • Protect your account, prevent fraud, and handle customer support inquiries.
  • Send push notifications regarding account activities and interaction updates.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or location data to third parties. Information may only be processed by trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting, database, and real-time call services) under strict data security protocols.

4. Location Permissions & Control

You can enable, modify, or disable location permissions at any time through your mobile device settings. Disabling location permissions may limit location-dependent features within Hidely.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active. You can request complete deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting our support team at support@kittuvirusstudio.in.

6. Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
