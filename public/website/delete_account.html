User Data Policy

Account & Data Deletion Request

You have full control over your data. You can request the permanent deletion of your Hidely account and all associated personal data using the form below or inside the Hidely mobile app.

What happens when your account is deleted:

  • Account Credentials: Your username, email address, password hash, and profile details will be permanently purged.
  • Content & Posts: All travel posts, photos, comments, and place reviews created by your account will be removed.
  • Location Data: Any cached location logs and saved places associated with your account ID will be wiped.
  • Retention Period: Deletion requests are processed immediately and fully completed within 30 days.
